Class Cutoffs & Duration

  • First Class: OGPA 3.70 or above
  • Second Upper: OGPA 3.30 – 3.69
  • Second Lower: OGPA 3.00 – 3.29
  • Pass: OGPA 2.00 – 2.99
  • Time Limits: Within 5 years for a Class; 8 years max to graduate. No intermediate exit point.

Pass Thresholds & Repeats

  • C+ (50%, 2.30 GP): Required for Sem 4–6 Elective Studios, ISRP (PL4702), and Final Studio (PL4802).
  • D (35%, 1.00 GP): Required for all other GPA modules.
  • 35% Component Hurdle: Below 35% in CA or WE = I; below 35% in both = F. Repeats capped at C (or C+ for studios/ISRP).
  • 6-Week Resubmission: Granted for ISRP/PL4802 if scoring D–C.

Attendance, Drop & Probation

  • Attendance: Minimum 80% per module required to sit exams.
  • Add/Drop Window: Written de-registration within 2 weeks of semester start.
  • Academic Concession (N): Notify SAR Examinations within 7 days for medical/compassionate absences.
  • Probation: SGPA 1.50–1.99 = Warning; below 1.50 = Probation (no extra load).
